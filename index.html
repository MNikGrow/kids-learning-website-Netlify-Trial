⬇ More about BodhiCubs

Free science games, brain games and mindful learning for kids

BodhiCubs is a free, ad-free learning website for children aged 6–12. Kids learn CBSE / NCERT science for Class 1 to Class 6 through picture quizzes and arcade-style games, train memory and focus in the Brain Gym, and take a mindful Calm Minute breathing break with Bodhi, our friendly baby elephant. It works in any browser on phones, tablets and computers — no sign-up and no download.

Science games for every class (CBSE / NCERT)

Each class has picture-based science adventures with hints, “Did you know?” facts, stars and stickers. Wrong answers become clues, so children learn by trying again — the way scientists do.

Class 1 science games (age 6–7)

  • My Body — eyes, ears, nose and more
  • Animal Friends — sounds, wings and homes
  • Yummy Garden — fruits and vegetables
  • Colours & Shapes — paint, mix and spot shapes
  • Weather Fun — rain, sun and rainbows

Class 2 science games (age 7–8)

  • Sky Watchers — Sun, Moon and stars
  • Healthy Habits — clean, strong and happy
  • Animal Homes — nests, hives and burrows
  • Green Friends — roots, leaves and fruits
  • Things That Move — planes, trains and ships

Class 3 science games (age 8–9)

  • Light & Shadows — torches, mirrors and shadows
  • Magnet Magic — push, pull and stick
  • Rocks & Soil — clay, sand and rocks
  • Food & Teeth — strong teeth, good food
  • Birds & Insects — wings, legs and feelers

Class 4 science games (age 9–10)

  • Plant Power — roots, water, sunlight and growth
  • Bee & Flower Lab — flowers, insects and pollination
  • Water Detective — sources, saving water and evaporation
  • Food Explorer — food sources and a healthy plate
  • Animal Detectives — ears, teeth, beaks, claws and shelters

Class 5 science games (age 10–11)

  • Super Senses — how animals see, hear and smell
  • Seed Stories — how seeds travel and grow
  • Water Wonders — evaporation, clouds and saving water
  • Food & Digestion — from mouth to tummy

Class 6 science games (age 11–12)

  • Living World — habitats, plants and animals
  • Healthy Eating — nutrients and balanced meals
  • Magnets & Motion — magnets, measuring and moving
  • Water & Materials — states of water and separation

Brain Gym: brain games for kids

Six short brain-training games adapt to your child’s level — they get harder after three right answers in a row and easier after mistakes. A skills chart shows growth in memory, logic, focus, speed and spatial thinking. Three minutes a day is enough.

  • 💡

    Memory Lights

    Kids remember a growing pattern of lights (working memory).

  • 🚂

    Pattern Train

    Kids find what comes next in a number or shape pattern (logic).

  • 🔍

    Odd One Out

    Kids spot the picture that does not belong (reasoning).

  • 🔢

    Quick Count

    Kids count and do quick sums against the clock (speed).

  • 👀

    Focus Finder

    Kids find the one different picture in a busy grid (attention).

  • 🌗

    Shadow Match

    Kids match a shape to its turned or flipped shadow (spatial thinking).

Mindful learning, inspired by the Bodhi tree

Bodhi means “awakening” — a mind that is wide awake, wise and kind. Long ago in Bodh Gaya, India, Prince Siddhartha sat calmly under a big peepal tree and thought deeply until he understood life. He became Gautama Buddha, and that tree is still called the Bodhi tree.

Our guide Bodhi is a baby elephant 🐘 — in the story of the Buddha’s birth, Queen Maya dreamt of a gentle white elephant.

BodhiCubs helps children grow the same way: with curious questions, calm minds and kind hearts. Every day Bodhi shares a new Bodhi thought about kindness, patience or noticing the world, and the Calm Minute teaches simple mindful breathing.

🌱 Be curious • 🧘 Stay calm • 💛 Be kind • 🙏 Be thankful

Made for every curious child — including families who love the Buddha’s gentle teachings of mindfulness and compassion.

Frequently asked questions

Is BodhiCubs free?

Yes. All science games, the Brain Gym and the Calm Minute are free, with no ads and no sign-up.

Which classes and ages is it for?

BodhiCubs covers science for Class 1 to Class 6, for children aged about 6 to 12.

Does it follow the CBSE / NCERT syllabus?

Yes. Topics follow NCERT / CBSE primary science (EVS) themes such as plants, animals, food, water, light, magnets and our body, together with global early-science standards.

Is it safe for my child?

Yes. There are no ads, no chat and no accounts. Your child’s name and progress stay only on your own device.

Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. BodhiCubs runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets, laptops and computers. You can also add it to your home screen like an app.

What is the Calm Minute?

A one-minute breathing break: kids breathe in and out with Bodhi the elephant as a circle grows and shrinks. It helps children settle and focus before learning.

Why is it called BodhiCubs?

“Bodhi” means awakening. Gautama Buddha found wisdom under the Bodhi tree in Bodh Gaya, India. BodhiCubs helps young learners — the cubs — grow curious minds, calm hearts and kind habits.

Which languages are available?

English is available now. Hindi and Marathi are coming soon.

Can teachers use it with a class?

Yes. Teachers can create homework links for any class, collect result codes, print worksheets and view results. School plans are coming soon.
