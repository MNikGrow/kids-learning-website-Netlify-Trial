Keep young brains busy during the break, without a screen

School breaks are lovely, but a few days in you may hear “I'm bored!”. This free pack gives children ages 4–9 real thinking games instead of more screen time. It works for any school holiday, a rainy afternoon, a long journey or a quiet hour at a family gathering, wherever you live.

Younger children (ages 4–5) can start with Odd One Out, What Comes Next, the maze and the drawing challenges. Older children (ages 6–9) will enjoy the Logic Detective puzzles, Number Pyramids, Secret Code Messages and picture sudoku. The Family Game Cards bring everyone together: cut them out, pick one and play. Most need nothing but you.

Everything is in one PDF. Low on color ink? Print the black & white pages in the middle of the file – kids can color them in themselves. Answers are at the end, so you can check quickly or let children mark their own work.
